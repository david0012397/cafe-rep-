The Repertwahr Legacy

Where Literature & Stage Meet Above the City

Born out of the creative ethos of the Repertwahr performing arts and theatre festival community in Lucknow, Cafe Repertwahr was created as a genuine 'third space' where literature, theatre, independent acoustic music, and culinary comfort intersect.

[Owner-Approved Brand Note]
"When we conceptualized Cafe Repertwahr, we didn't just want another eatery in Lucknow. We wanted a living, breathing third space that honors the pulse of our Repertwahr theatre festival movement. A place where you can sit undisturbed for hours with a novel, strike up a conversation with an unfamiliar artist over pour-over coffee, and watch dusk turn into golden fairy lights above Faizabad Road. Whether you are writing your first script or sharing a meal with old friends, our rooftop is your sanctuary."
CR

Parking Information

On-site parking lot and free street parking available along Kala Niketan. Note that peak weekend evenings get busy; ride-sharing or metro access is recommended.

Accessibility Disclosure

Ground parking is accessible. However, the cafe is located on the 3rd floor and currently does not have an elevator / wheelchair-accessible entrance. Staircase access required.
